# KET (A2) syllabus for daily reading

Each day has 3 texts. Pick them yourself from the lists below — no teacher choice needed.
Look at the last 10 day files (`content/days/*.json`) first and follow the rotation rules.

## Rotation rules

- The 3 texts of a day use 3 different **topics**, 3 different **genres** and 3 different **grammar points**.
- A grammar point does not come back within 10 days; work through the list in order, then start again
  (second time round, make the practice harder: more fill items, mixed with earlier points).
- A topic does not come back within 5 days. A genre at most once a day.
- Every text is original writing in the style of A2 coursebooks and Cambridge A2 Key papers
  (topic, length, sentence patterns, question types). Do not copy published textbook or exam texts.
- Characters are teenagers, families, teachers, local people. Give them international names.
- One text a day may be a real-world fact text (an animal, a place, an invention, a festival);
  check facts with WebSearch and add `sources`. Fiction, emails and interviews need no sources.

## Topics (Cambridge A2 Key topic list)

Clothes · Daily life · Entertainment & media · Food & drink · Health & exercise · Hobbies & leisure ·
House & home · Language · People & family · Personal feelings & experiences · Places & buildings ·
School & study · Services (bank, post office, library) · Shopping · Social interaction · Sport ·
Technology · The natural world · Transport · Travel & holidays · Weather · Work & jobs

Use the topic name above in the `topic` field.

## Genres (`genre` field)

| genre | shape | KET link |
|---|---|---|
| Article | magazine/website text with a title, 5–6 paragraphs | Reading Part 3, 4 |
| Story | past-tense story with a problem and an ending | Writing Part 7 model |
| Email | 2–3 emails in a thread (Hi …, / Love, …), each a paragraph | Reading Part 2, Writing Part 6 |
| Interview | magazine interview: questions in bold-free text, then answers, one Q&A per paragraph | Reading Part 3 |
| Three people | three short texts by three teenagers on the same subject (e.g. "My weekend job") | Reading Part 2 matching |
| Notices & messages | 5–7 short real-world texts (notice, text message, sign, note, post-it), one per paragraph, with a short framing paragraph first | Reading Part 1 |
| Blog post | a teenager's blog about a trip, hobby or event | Reading Part 3 |

## Grammar points (in order)

1. be and have got · there is / there are
2. Present simple · adverbs of frequency
3. Present continuous vs present simple
4. Countable / uncountable · some / any · much / many / a lot of
5. Past simple of be · was / were
6. Past simple: regular and irregular verbs
7. Past simple questions and negatives
8. Past continuous with when / while
9. Comparative adjectives
10. Superlative adjectives · as … as
11. can / can't · could for past ability
12. must / have to / don't have to
13. should / shouldn't for advice
14. going to for plans · present continuous for arrangements
15. will / won't for predictions and offers
16. Present perfect with ever / never
17. Present perfect with just / already / yet
18. Present perfect vs past simple
19. Prepositions of time (in / on / at)
20. Prepositions of place and movement
21. Possessives: my / mine, 's, whose
22. Object pronouns and reflexive pronouns
23. Question words (who, which, how often, how long…)
24. Adverbs of manner (quickly, well)
25. -ing and to-infinitive after verbs (like doing, want to do)
26. First conditional (if + present, will)
27. Relative clauses with who / which / that
28. Linking words: and, but, so, because, then, after that
29. Too / enough · very
30. Passive: is made / was built (simple forms only)
